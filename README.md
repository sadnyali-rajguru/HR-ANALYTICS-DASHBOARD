# HR-ANALYTICS-DASHBOARD


# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Title / Headline

**👥 HR Analytics Dashboard**
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee data, attrition, workforce demographics, job roles, departments, salaries, and employee performance—helping organizations make data-driven HR decisions through dynamic visualizations.

---

# 2. Short Description / Purpose

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report that provides a comprehensive analysis of employee data, attrition trends, salary distribution, job roles, departments, employee demographics, and workforce characteristics. It enables HR teams and management to identify workforce trends, understand employee attrition, and make informed business decisions through data visualization.

---

# 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Used to design and build the interactive dashboard.
* 🗄️ **SQL** – Used for data querying, filtering, analysis, and extracting relevant employee insights.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Performed data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Created calculated measures, KPIs, and dynamic calculations.
* 📈 **Data Visualization** – Used charts, KPI cards, slicers, tables, and other visuals to present HR insights.

# 5. Features / Highlights

## • Business Problem

Organizations generate large amounts of employee data across departments, job roles, salaries, and employee characteristics. Without an analytical dashboard, it becomes difficult to answer important HR questions such as:

* What is the overall employee attrition rate?
* Which departments have the highest attrition?
* Which job roles have the most employees?
* How does salary vary across different job roles?
* Which age groups have higher attrition?
* How does overtime affect employee attrition?
* Which departments have the highest workforce?
* What factors are associated with employee turnover?

---

## • Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard is designed to:

* Monitor overall HR performance through KPIs.
* Analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.
* Understand employee demographics.
* Compare departments and job roles.
* Analyze salary and employee experience.
* Identify factors contributing to employee attrition.
* Support data-driven HR decision-making.
* Enable interactive filtering for deeper analysis.

## • Business Impact & Insights

### 📉 Attrition Reduction

Identify departments, job roles, and employee groups with higher attrition rates to support employee retention strategies.

### 💰 Compensation Analysis

Analyze salary patterns across job roles and departments to support better compensation planning.

### 👥 Workforce Planning

Understand employee distribution across departments, roles, age groups, and experience levels for effective workforce planning.

### 🎯 Employee Retention

Identify factors such as overtime, job satisfaction, and tenure that may be associated with employee attrition.

### 📊 HR Decision Making

Provide HR teams and management with a centralized dashboard for monitoring workforce KPIs and supporting strategic decisions.

### 🚀 Data-Driven HR Strategy

Use SQL and Power BI to transform raw employee data into meaningful insights that can support better HR policies and workforce management.
